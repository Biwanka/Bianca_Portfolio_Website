Medication Dispenser

3D

Watch Animation

(Bianca Gauthier, blender 3d,2025)

Concept Description

My animation explores the tension between childlike innocence and the darker reality of chronic illness. The piece takes the familiar imagery of a retro bubblegum dispenser and subverts it by replacing candy with prescription medication. The loop structure reflects the repetitive, mundane routine of taking medication every day, something that has always been part of my life, but has gotten worse since I was eighteen. The idea originated from a joke my twin brother made about my “side dish of medication” with my meal. I found that moment both funny but with a messed-up nuance, and it became the foundation for this work: an everyday ritual that looks harmless on the surface but carries much heavier implications, slightly unsettling when thinking deeper about it. I wanted a very quirky, colourful, childlike visual for the loop.
